# GG 0.1.20

### New
- **Accounts view**: a third way to see your characters, grouped by account. Each card shows at a glance who the character is, what's left this week (Great Vault, Heroic and Mythic raid progress) and their key season currencies.
- **Invites through GG**: Request invite now asks your roster's leader or assistant through GG, and they invite you automatically. The whisper is still used as a fallback, and with players on older versions.
- **Auto-accept**: after you click Request invite, the invite from your roster's group is accepted for you. You can turn it off in Settings > Pop-ups.
- **Silent pill**: Not now shrinks the roster alert to a small pill next to the minimap instead of hiding it. If you were already in that group this week, you only get the pill, without sound.
- **Hidden characters**: when not all your characters fit in the window, a tab on its edge shows how many are hidden. Click it to scroll.
- The window now opens with the character you're logged in on in view.
- **New Gear window**: upgrade tracks, missing enchants and gems per item, and your crafting cap based on your crests.

### Improvements
- New window look: rounded corners and a single top bar with sliding tabs, framed in your interface color.
- The window resizes smoothly between tabs and its top-left corner never moves. You can turn it off with "Animate tab changes" in Settings > Window.
- Your main character gets a gold frame and a star, and how old each account's data is now shows on its account chip.
- Long character names, realms and guilds are no longer cut off.
- Theme picker with previews of all 6 themes.
- Bonus roll details: one line per character.
- The lockout alert when joining a raid is shorter and only lists what you're saved to.
- Not now is now a clear red button.
